Privacy Policy

Green Future NGO values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Information We Collect

  • Personal details such as name, email, and phone number when you contact us or donate.
  • Payment information for donations (processed securely through our payment provider).
  • Non-personal data such as browser type and site usage statistics.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process donations, communicate project updates, and improve our services. We will never sell your data to third parties.

Data Protection

We implement security measures to protect your information against unauthorised access or misuse. All sensitive data is encrypted and securely stored.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at info@greenfuture.org.za.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, please contact us at info@greenfuture.org.za or call +27 68 210 1354.
