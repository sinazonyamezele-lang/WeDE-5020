Our Projects

Initiatives making a real difference in communities and ecosystems across South Africa.

Reforestation

Tree Planting

We plant trees to restore forests, improve air quality, and rebuild natural habitats. Working with local communities, we've planted over 5,000 trees across South Africa.

Education

Recycling Education

We teach communities how to recycle and reduce waste through workshops, school programmes, and awareness campaigns — building habits that last a lifetime.

Community Action

Community Clean-Ups

We organise clean-up events to keep our neighbourhoods, rivers, and natural spaces pristine. Every event brings the community together for a cleaner environment.
