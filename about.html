About Us

Founded in 2018, Green Future NGO works to protect the environment and promote sustainable living across South Africa. We believe that lasting change begins at community level. Explore our active work on the Projects page, or see how you can help on our Donate page.

🎯 Our Mission

To educate communities about protecting the environment and encourage sustainable practices that last.

🌍 Our Vision

A cleaner and greener future for everyone — today and for generations to come.
